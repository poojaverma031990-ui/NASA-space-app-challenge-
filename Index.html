How It Works

step 1

Select 35 Cities

You pick a city. The page fetches real temperature, rainfall and sunlight for the most recent published week from NASA POWER — free, no key, no login.

step 2

Qwen AI Converts Numbers to Sound Rules

Qwen AI helped write the rules: warmer air → higher pitch, more rain → more drop-notes, stronger sunlight → a faster walk.

step 3

Browser Plays Sound

Web Audio oscillators sing the rules in your browser. Nothing pre-recorded, nothing downloaded — your planet, played live.
